# Race committee quick guide

Everything you need on race day. Results are scored for you: enter start and finish times and the app does the rest.

## Signing in
1. Open the club's results site and click **Sign in** (top right).
2. Use the username and password you were given. The first time, you will be asked to choose your own password
   (15 or more characters; a few ordinary words works well).
3. You land on **Race committee**, which lists the series and their races. Use the menu to move around.

Forgot your password? Ask a site administrator to reset it. You will pick a new one at your next sign-in.

## Before the season (once)
- **Boats & ratings:** make sure every boat is listed with its rating(s). Boats can also be imported from a spreadsheet.
- **Series:** create the series (name, season, fleets, discards, scoring). *Low Point* is the standard. Choose
  *Pursuit start* here if the races use staggered pursuit starts. Add the race dates, or generate weekly dates.
- **Entry list:** open the series and choose which boats are entered and in which fleet.
- **Marks & courses:** only if you use courses from the course chart.

## On race day
1. **Race committee** > pick the race.
2. For each fleet, set the **course** (or type in a distance), the **rating type**, and the **start time**, then
   click **Set start**. Anything marked with a red asterisk must be filled in before it will save.
3. Set the race status to **sailed** once the first start goes, so results appear on the public site.
4. Boats not on the list (guests, late entries)? Use **Add a boat for this race**.

### Pursuit race
The race screen shows a blue **Pursuit start times** list: the slowest boat starts at the first gun and each other
boat starts at the time shown. Check it just before the first gun, because adding a boat or rating can move the
times. The first boat across the finish line wins.

### Recording finishes
- Type the finish time for each boat (hours:minutes:seconds), or press **Now** as the boat crosses the line.
- If a boat did not finish, pick a code instead: **DNS** (did not start), **DNF** (did not finish), **OCS**
  (over the line early), **DSQ**, **RET** and so on.
- Spot a mistake? Change the time or code and save; scores recalculate immediately.
- Missing rating or distance? The app will tell you what to add. A boat's rating can be added right on this screen.
- A boat that was in the wrong fleet can be moved from the boat's menu (⋯), for this race or the rest of the series.
  The same menu removes a boat from the race.

### Penalties and protests
- Give a penalty or redress from the boat's row. **Always add a short reason**: it is shown on the public results.
- Use **Protests** on the race screen to log a protest and its decision. The race cannot be marked final while a
  protest is still open.

### After the race
1. Check the **Results preview** at the bottom of the race screen.
2. Tick **Results final** when you are happy. Until then the public sees "Provisional".
3. The public results page has **Print podium sheet** and a CSV download for the club.

## Fees (only if your club charges entry fees)
Under the series, record who has paid and how much (for the whole series or one race). A boat that has not paid is
not scored and does not appear on the public results. Members can be set to race free in selected races.

## Crew board
**Crew board** in the menu is where you approve skippers who ask for a login (they pick their boat; you set a
temporary password) and clear out spam sign-ups. Skippers can only edit their own boat's crew openings. They
cannot change results, ratings or fees.

## If something looks wrong
- **Results did not update:** check the race status is *sailed* and each fleet has a start saved.
- **A boat shows no points:** it probably has no rating for that rating type, or has not paid (if fees apply).
- **Wrong rating used:** edit the boat's ratings from the race screen, then use **Update start** to re-apply them.
- **Locked out after several wrong passwords:** wait 15 minutes, or ask an administrator to unlock you.
- Anything else: ask the site administrator.
